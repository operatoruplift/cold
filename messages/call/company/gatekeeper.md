---
title: Gatekeeper
category: General
when: A receptionist, front desk or office manager picks up. Be polite, be specific, ask for help.
---
## Opener
Hi, this is {{sender_first_name}} from {{sender_company}}. I'm hoping you can help me. Who looks after [the website / marketing / bookings] at {{company}}?

## If they ask what it's about
We {{offer}}, and I'd like to see if it's relevant for {{company}}. Who would be the best person for that?

## If the owner is out
No problem. When is the best time to catch them? And is there a direct line or an email, so I can send a short note first?

## Leave a message
Could you let them know {{sender_first_name}} from {{sender_company}} called about {{outcome}}? My number is {{sender_phone}}. Thank you, I appreciate your help.
