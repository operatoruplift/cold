## Opener
Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{sender_first_name}} with {{sender_company}}. The reason for my call: we work with {{audience}}, and I wanted to see if {{company}} is running into the same thing they are. Have you got 30 seconds?

## The pitch
Most {{audience}} we talk to {{pain}}. We {{offer}}. {{proof}}

## Discovery
- How are you handling that today?
- How many [jobs / leads / calls] a week would you guess slip through?
- If you fixed it, what would that be worth over a year?
- Who else would weigh in on a decision like this?

## Close
It sounds like it's worth a proper look. The next step is a 15-minute walkthrough. Does [day] or [day] suit you better?

## Not the decision maker
Thanks for telling me. Who would be the best person to speak with about this, and what's the best way to reach them?
