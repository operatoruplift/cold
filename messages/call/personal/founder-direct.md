## Opener
Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{sender_first_name}}, I run {{sender_company}}. I know I'm calling out of the blue. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and you can tell me if it's worth keeping going?

## Why I called
{{trigger}} I {{offer}}, and most {{audience}} I talk to {{pain}}.

## The question
Is that something you run into at {{company}}, or have you got it covered?

## If it's a problem
- How are you handling it today?
- Roughly how often does it happen in a normal week?
- What would it be worth to fix?

{{proof}} I'd like to show you how that would look for {{company}}. Does [day] or [day] work for 15 minutes?

## If they have it covered
Good to hear. Out of curiosity, what are you using? (Listen.) Fair enough. Would it be all right if I checked back in a couple of months?

## Close
Book it while you're on the phone: "What's the best email for the invite?" Read the time back to them before you hang up.
