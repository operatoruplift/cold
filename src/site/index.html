/ Our principle

Cold outreach.
Warm conversations.

The best outbound doesn't feel robotic. It feels relevant, timely and human.

  1. Context

    Every message starts from something true about the person you're writing to.

  2. Control

    AI drafts. You decide. Nothing goes out unless it fits your rules.

  3. Respect

    Daily caps, quiet hours and an opt-out in every email. One “stop” ends it everywhere.

  4. Transparency

    No fake stats and no fake familiarity. Sources are linked and every send is logged.

Relevance beats volume.

/ Questions

Straight
answers.

What is COLD?

An AI outbound workspace. It finds prospects, drafts personalized emails and DMs, prepares your calls and sorts the replies, with you in control of every send.

Does COLD send messages on its own?

Only the email steps you approve, inside the daily caps and send windows you set. DMs are lined up for you to send yourself, because LinkedIn, X and Instagram don't allow automated messages. Calls are made by you.

Does it make AI voice calls?

No. COLD prepares you for the call: the opener, the questions, the likely objections. You do the talking. In the US, AI-generated voices count as artificial voices that need prior consent, and a cold call deserves a person anyway.

How does it keep outreach respectful?

Every email carries your postal address and a one-click unsubscribe. Opt-outs, bounces and “not interested” replies stop a sequence on every channel. Daily caps and send windows keep the volume human.

Where does my data live?

On your own computer or server, and in your Twenty CRM if you use one. COLD is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.
